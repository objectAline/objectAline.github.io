Privacy Policy

qm-dev · last updated 2 September 2026

qm-dev is a personal, self-hosted assistant operated by one individual for their own use. It has no users other than its operator, collects nothing from anyone else, and is not offered as a service.

Data it accesses

With the operator's explicit Google consent, qm-dev reads and acts on that same operator's own Google data: Gmail messages and drafts, Calendar events, Drive file contents and metadata, Sheets, Docs and Slides content, and Tasks. It requests read-only access to Drive and cannot delete Drive files; it cannot delete or trash Gmail messages.

How the data is used

Solely to answer the operator's own requests in their own private conversation — for example summarising a calendar, searching mail, or reading a spreadsheet. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, training, or any purpose beyond answering the request in front of it.

Where the data goes

  • The operator's own machine. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest on the operator's server and are never displayed in chat or written to logs.
  • Anthropic. qm-dev is driven by a large language model. The content needed to answer a request — which may include the text of mail, calendar entries, or documents — is sent to Anthropic's API for processing, under Anthropic's terms. No other third party receives it.

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with anyone else, and is not transferred to any other party for any purpose.

Retention

Google data is fetched on demand to answer a request and is not maintained as a separate copy beyond the conversation history and any working notes the operator keeps on their own machine. Deleting the conversation removes it.

Limited Use

qm-dev's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately and stored tokens stop working.
